# Algorithm references

The implementation uses and should be cited alongside the relevant primary
methods and software papers:

- Kovács, Zucker & Mazeh (2002), Box-fitting Least Squares.
- Hippke & Heller (2019), Transit Least Squares.
- Lightkurve collaboration, TESS light-curve analysis software.
- Foreman-Mackey et al., `exoplanet` probabilistic transit modelling.
- Salvatier, Wiecki & Fonnesbeck (2016), PyMC Bayesian inference.
- Kumar et al., ArviZ diagnostics and visualization.

Thresholds and detrending windows remain AstroTransit configuration decisions;
their justification must come from the benchmark reports, not from citations.
