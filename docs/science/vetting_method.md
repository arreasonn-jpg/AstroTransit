# Vetting method

Vetting combines transit consistency, odd/even behaviour, secondary-eclipse
checks, stellar context and cross-sector evidence. It rejects or flags plausible
false positives; it does not prove a planet without external evidence.

Cross-sector states are `single_sector_candidate`, `multi_sector_consistent`,
`multi_sector_inconsistent` and `multi_sector_incomplete`. Failure taxonomy is
serialized in the candidate record.
