# ASTROTRANSIT: PRESCREENED TESS CANDIDATE WATCHLIST NOTE

## Critical Disclaimer & Limitations
1. **UNCONFIRMED CANDIDATES**: All candidates in this package are **unconfirmed planet candidates**. TESS photometry alone cannot confirm a planet. Ground-based high-resolution Adaptive Optics (AO) imaging and Radial Velocity (RV) spectroscopy are required to rule out background blended binaries and measure planet masses.
2. **HEURISTIC VETTING**: Reported FPP metrics are proxy diagnostic scores, not formal statistical validations (e.g., TRICERATOPS).
3. **NON-TOI STATUS**: Non-TOI indicates targets not currently listed in the official NASA TOI list. They may represent unflagged signals or targets skipped due to stellar/instrumental variability.
