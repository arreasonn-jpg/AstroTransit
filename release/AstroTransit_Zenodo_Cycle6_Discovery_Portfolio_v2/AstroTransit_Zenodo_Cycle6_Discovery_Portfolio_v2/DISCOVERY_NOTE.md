## 1. Executive Summary & Vetting Discipline
This evidence package provides empirical TESS photometry fits and MCMC parameter estimations for verified **Class A Non-TOI exoplanet candidates**.

To maintain rigorous scientific standards, all targets flagged with secondary eclipses, eclipsing binary (EB) signatures, or significant stellar variability during FPP vetting (e.g., TIC 289972535) have been **explicitly purged** from the candidate discovery list.
